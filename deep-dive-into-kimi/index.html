Yin Yang · First Edition

Deep Dive into Kimi K3

A three-volume technical guide to Kimi K3 as a model, a learning process, and an executable system. Follow the mechanisms from sparse experts and hybrid attention through reinforcement learning and agents to accelerator kernels, distributed training, and long-context serving.

Volume I · Chapters 1–16

Architecture and Pretraining

Build from a complete Transformer decoder to KDA, Gated MLA, Attention Residuals, Stable LatentMoE, MoonViT, and the integrated K3 model. Study Muon, training stability, long-context learning, and native multimodal pretraining.

Paperback Hardcover Kindle

Volume II · Chapters 17–31

Post-Training and Agent Intelligence

Connect supervised cold start and reinforcement learning to reasoning, rewards, task synthesis, multimodal agents, and agent swarms. Follow structured generation, tool execution, durable context, coding, kernel optimization, and agent evaluation.

Paperback Hardcover Kindle

Volume III · Chapters 32–46

Systems and Deployment

Move from GPU and parallelism foundations to FlashKDA, routed computation, MoonEP, distributed execution, and memory-efficient training. Trace online weight publication, million-token RL, Mooncake serving, hybrid prefix caching, fleet recovery, low-precision execution, and speculative decoding.

Paperback Hardcover Kindle
