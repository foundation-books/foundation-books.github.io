Deep Dive into DeepSeek

Deep Dive into DeepSeek

A two-volume technical guide to DeepSeek models and the systems that run them - from DeepSeekMoE, MLA, V3, R1, V4, Coder, and Janus to serving, FlashMLA, DeepGEMM, DeepEP, DualPipe, 3FS, numerical correctness, and measured runtime evidence.

Buy Volume I Buy Volume II Companion Code

Volume I

Models, Training, and Reasoning

Volume I builds from request and cost foundations into DeepSeekMoE, MLA, V3, R1, V4, Coder, multimodal systems, OCR, and the evidence needed to compare architecture and training claims responsibly.

Paperback Hardcover Kindle

Volume II

Systems, Kernels, and Integration

Volume II follows requests through serving runtimes, accelerator kernels, expert-parallel communication, pipeline and storage systems, quantization, long context, replay, and measured execution.
